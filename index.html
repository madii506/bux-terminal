FAQ

Questions players ask.

What is BUX?

The first in-game currency for GTA, and a terminal to spend it in. You trade fresh pump.fun launches and any Solana memecoin, set limit orders, hedge with perps on Hyperliquid, follow the biggest traders, set alerts and track your bag, all from one screen.

Is BUX part of GTA, Rockstar or Take-Two?

No. BUX is a fan-made memecoin and trading terminal. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. GTA is their trademark.

Does BUX hold my money?

No. Your SOL and coins stay in your Solana wallet and every swap is signed there. Your margin stays in your own Hyperliquid account. There is no BUX account and no deposit to us.

How do trades work?

Swaps go through Jupiter, which finds the best route across Solana, pump.fun curves included. You see the quote, your wallet signs once, and Jupiter sends it. Perps go straight to Hyperliquid.

How do limit orders work?

Pick Limit in the buy or sell box and set your price. Jupiter keeps the order onchain and fills it when the coin trades at your price, even with this tab closed. Cancel any time and the funds come back to your wallet. Jupiter's minimum is about $5 per order.

What is the Whales page?

The public Hyperliquid leaderboard, ranked by the window you pick, plus every open position of any trader on it. Copy or fade opens the same perp in the terminal; you choose the size and sign it yourself. You can also watch any Solana wallet, priced live. It's read-only: nothing is signed and nothing is stored.

What is the session key?

Hyperliquid lets you approve an "agent" key that can place and cancel orders for your account but can never withdraw. BUX creates one in your browser, your wallet approves it once, and it stays in this browser only. Forget it any time from the wallet menu.

What does hedging actually do?

A short gains when its price falls. If your coin and the perp fall together, the short pays back part of what the bag lost. If your coin pumps while the perp is flat, you keep most of the pump. The hedge costs you some upside and pays or earns funding.

My coin has no perp. Can I still hedge?

Yes, with SOL or another meme perp as a market hedge. It covers the "whole market dumps" risk, not the "this coin rugs" risk. The terminal says which one you're getting.

Which wallets work?

For memes: any Solana wallet that supports Wallet Standard, like Phantom, Solflare or Backpack. For perps: any EVM wallet, like Phantom, MetaMask or Rabby, with USDC on Hyperliquid.

What does it cost?

Jupiter's swap fee and Hyperliquid's trading fee, the same as on their own sites. BUX adds nothing.

What can go wrong?

Memecoins can go to zero. Leverage can be liquidated if the perp pumps far enough. Funding can turn against a short. A market hedge can fall less than your coin. Only trade what you can lose.
